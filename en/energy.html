Services

From a single air-conditioner to a full industrial installation

Every space has different needs. We start with a site visit and calculations, so the system is exactly as big as it needs to be: not undersized, not oversized.

Air-conditioning

Split and multi-split inverter units, central VRV/VRF systems. Advice on capacity (BTU), energy class and noise.

Heat pumps

Heating, cooling and domestic hot water with air-to-water heat pumps, combined with underfloor heating or fan coils.

Ventilation & heat recovery

Mechanical ventilation for clean air without energy losses, in homes and commercial spaces.

Industrial climate control

Air-handling units, ductwork and temperature & humidity control for production areas and warehouses.

Energy upgrades

Replacing old systems with more efficient ones, for lower bills and better comfort.

Maintenance & service

Regular checks, cleaning, performance testing and repairs, for systems that keep working properly for years.
