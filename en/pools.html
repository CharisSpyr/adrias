The adrias technology

All the strengths of concrete, none of its limits

adrias pools have everything that makes traditional construction stand out: a heavy, solid structure, a long service life, earthquake resistance and good water circulation. On top of that we add perfect watertightness, unlimited designs and fast construction.

At the heart of the system is Betoform: a one-piece, pre-coloured fibreglass wall with UV treatment. It acts both as the formwork for the concrete and as the finished surface, smooth to the touch, with no liner needed.

  • Built in around 15 days
  • 10-year warranty on structure and watertightness
  • Approved structural design and circulation & filtration design
  • No standard sizes: every pool is designed for its site

FAQ

What to know before you start

How long does construction take?

An adrias pool is usually built in around 15 days from the start of works. Before we begin, you get a clear written schedule.

Is the pool fully licensed? Is a design study needed?

Yes. adrias pools are designed and built in line with Greek legislation, with a structural design approved by the planning authorities and an approved mechanical/electrical design for water circulation and filtration.

Why concrete and not a liner pool?

Concrete gives a heavy, solid, earthquake-resistant structure with a long service life. With the Betoform wall the surface is already watertight and smooth, so you are free to choose the finish (paint, tiles, mosaic, quartz) without depending on a plastic membrane.

Are running and maintenance costs high?

Not necessarily. Good water circulation, effective filtration and automatic cleaning keep chemicals and maintenance time to a minimum. We give you a running-cost estimate for your own pool.

Can the pool fit my budget?

Yes. Since there are no standard sizes, the shape, size, finishes and equipment are chosen with you to match your needs and your budget.
