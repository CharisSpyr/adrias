Who we are

A family company that builds things to last

adrias was founded in 2004 in Aigio, growing out of our long experience (since 1992) in designing, building and installing concrete swimming pools.

With know-how from our collaboration with leading French pool manufacturers, carefully chosen materials and our own engineering experience, we build pools entirely from concrete, without a liner. We also design and install air-conditioning, heating and ventilation systems for homes, businesses and industrial sites.

  • Approved structural and mechanical/electrical design
  • One team from design to maintenance
  • Partner network across Greece and Cyprus
